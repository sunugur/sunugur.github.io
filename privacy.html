Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

rclone-mini is used only by its owner to store the owner's own encrypted backup files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google Drive access is used solely to upload, list, verify and delete those backup files.
  • No data is collected from, or shared with, anyone else. No analytics, advertising or third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
